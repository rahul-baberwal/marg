Privacy policy

Marg: Fake GPS & Route Mocker (package com.rahulbaberwal.marg). Effective 19 November 2026.

Marg is developed by Rahul Baberwal (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: Marg has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and I don’t receive or store any personal data about you.

Your location

Marg does not read, record or send your real location. The positions it produces are calculated on your phone from the start and destination you choose, and are handed to Android’s mock location system. They are not uploaded anywhere by Marg.

Android requires the location permission for an app to run a location foreground service, which is why Marg asks for it.

Network requests Marg makes

Marg needs the internet for two things. Both go to public OpenStreetMap services, not to my servers.

Purpose Service What is sent
Calculate a route OpenStreetMap-Germany routing (routing.openstreetmap.de) The start and destination coordinates you picked and the travel mode. Your IP address is visible to the service, as with any web request.
Show the map OpenStreetMap tile server (tile.openstreetmap.org) Which map tiles to load for the area you are viewing, and your IP address.

These services have their own policies. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy. I don’t control them.

Information stored on your device

The route you select and your settings (such as speed) are kept in the app’s memory while it runs. Marg does not create an account or save your history on my servers, because I don’t operate any.

Permissions

  • Location: required by Android to run the mock location service. Not sent anywhere.
  • Notifications: to show the “Marg is mocking your location” notification with a Stop button.
  • Foreground service (location): keeps the simulation running when the app is in the background.
  • Internet: for routing and map tiles, as described above.

Sharing, selling and advertising

I don’t sell or share personal data. Marg contains no advertising and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Children

Marg is a developer and testing tool and is not directed at children under 13. I don’t knowingly collect information from children.

Responsible use

Marg is intended for app development, testing, demos and learning. You are responsible for following the laws and the terms of any app or service you use it with.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: im@rahulbaberwal.com. More about the developer: rahulbaberwal.com.
